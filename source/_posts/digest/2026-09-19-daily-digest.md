Today's strongest signal is convergence: direct-to-device satellite networks are moving toward real broadband service while regulators prepare the mid-band spectrum pipeline for 6G. At the same time, AI products are specializing by profession and science, and governments are accelerating oversight.

## Amazon Leo proposes a 5,100-satellite direct-to-device network

Amazon says it has filed with the FCC to launch and operate more than 5,100 LEO satellites for a direct-to-device service spanning mobile data, messaging, and emergency communications. Deployment could begin as early as 2028. This is separate from Leo's first-generation broadband constellation, which Amazon says now has nearly 400 satellites in orbit and more than 100 launches secured.

Source: [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/policy-news-views/united-nations-general-assembly-2026-amazon-ai-sustainability)

## Starlink and Fastweb complete Italy's first satellite video call

Fastweb completed a video call from the Umbrian Apennines using an ordinary 4G smartphone and Starlink Mobile's direct-to-cell network. Video is a much more demanding demonstration than text messaging, making the test a meaningful marker on the path from emergency coverage to broadband-style service outside terrestrial footprints.

Source: [Starlink on X](https://x.com/Starlink/status/2100678883532312942)

## FCC lines up three more bands for future auctions

An adviser to FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said notices of proposed rulemaking are expected for 7.125–7.4 GHz, 1.675–1.695 GHz, and 2.7–2.8 GHz, with auctions targeted for 2027. Together with the scheduled Upper C-band auction, the work could create a broader pipeline of contiguous mid-band spectrum for rising mobile demand and eventual 6G deployment.

Source: [Broadband Breakfast](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/fcc-adviser-says-more-spectrum-auction-nprms-coming-soon/)

## OpenAI launches Astra for Law

OpenAI introduced Astra for Law, a legal configuration of GPT-6 Astra designed to support lawyers and legal-technology firms with domain-specific tools, settings, and context. It also announced 26 partner-built and 47 community plugins. Selected firms will receive Trusted Access first, with API availability planned later.

Source: [OpenAI on X](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2100679992720142459)

## DeepMind opens AlphaGenome Atlas

AlphaGenome Atlas is now freely available to researchers working on disease-linked genetic variation. DeepMind says Atlas maps more than 2,500 regulatory patterns across hundreds of cell types, exposing DNA sequences that behave like switches and volume controls for gene activity. Broad Institute, the University of Exeter, and other research groups are already using it.

Source: [Google DeepMind on X](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2100586760036143330)

## MONAI turns pediatric heart scans into 3D models in seconds

At Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, open-source AI built with MONAI transforms CT scans, MRIs, and 3D ultrasound into precise, patient-specific heart models. NVIDIA says a workflow that previously required more than six hours of manual modeling can now run in seconds, helping clinicians compare device fit and plan procedures around unique anatomy.

Source: [NVIDIA on X](https://x.com/nvidia/status/2100935381206049248)

## California accelerates frontier-AI oversight

Governor Gavin Newsom signed an executive order directing experts to recommend stronger AI safety and security rules within two months. The group will examine independent monitoring of frontier labs, safety plans, audits, and a possible emergency shutdown mechanism. California's influence matters because most leading U.S. AI labs and much of their infrastructure are based there, although federal-state authority remains contested.

Source: [ABC7 / CNN](https://abc7news.com/post/california-gov-gavin-newsom-signs-executive-order-consider-ai-regulation-including-proposal-kill-switch/19847515/)

## OpenClaw 2026.9.5 improves live operations

OpenClaw's latest release adds atomic updates, plugin hot reload, conversation sharing, expanded GPT Live support, and shared browsing. The release focuses less on flashy model capability and more on the operational layer needed to keep persistent multi-agent systems updated, observable, and collaborative.

Source: [Peter Steinberger on X](https://x.com/steipete/status/2101157545658503462)

## Research Radar

### STR-Agent: An LLM-Driven Agent for QoS-Aware Routing in LEO Satellite Networks

Bowen Lu, Mugen Peng, Yaohua Sun, Hongyu Wang, Kerui Guo, and Wenjia Xu present an agent that converts natural-language service requests into structured routing requirements, executes routing tools, and adapts policy through reflection. In Walker-Delta simulations, supervised fine-tuning raised intent accuracy from 45.4% to 92.45%, while delay fell by as much as 60% relative to DQ-Dijkstra. The record links the work to IEEE ICCC.

Source: [arXiv:2609.20347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20347)

### JANUS: Denial-of-Service Attack Against Beam Hopping in LEO Satellite Networks

Yuval Aviv, Roee Idan, Roy Peled, Asaf Shabtai, and Yuval Elovici identify a new security weakness in demand-driven beam hopping. A small botnet sends legitimate traffic in carefully selected non-victim cells, distorting the scheduler's demand estimate so capacity moves away from the targeted area. JANUS caused complete denial for up to about 95% of tested victims against one scheduler and excluded victims from about 92% of DRL scheduling decisions.

Source: [arXiv:2609.19977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19977)

### RUN-O-RAN: An O-RAN-Native Architecture Enabling Cooperative Uplink Localization

Viola Bernazzoli, Alberto Ceresoli, and Ilario Filippini demonstrate an xApp that coordinates standard sounding reference signals across serving and neighboring base stations. More than 150,000 SRS transmissions validated meter-level network-side localization without changing commercial user equipment or existing 3GPP signaling, offering a practical route toward network-native ISAC positioning.

Source: [arXiv:2609.20640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20640)
