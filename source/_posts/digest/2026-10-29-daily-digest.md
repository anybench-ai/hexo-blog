Today’s signal is continuous agency: frontier models are learning to work for days and simulate interactive worlds, enterprise software is moving into conversational workspaces, and wireless networks are being redesigned for traffic generated by agents, sensors, and machines.

## Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 4 Carbon

Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon as its next frontier model, emphasizing state-of-the-art cybersecurity performance and the ability to sustain complex, long-horizon coding tasks for days. DeepMind is initially working with a limited group of cyber defenders so their feedback can shape guardrails before access expands. Carbon launches at the same introductory API price as Gemini 4 Argon: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a 95% discount for cached inputs.

Source: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model/)

## Genie 4 turns prompts into interactive worlds with native audio

DeepMind’s Genie 4 generates photorealistic environments that users can navigate and change in real time with keyboard controls and natural-language prompts. It adds native ambient sound, music, and interactive dialogue; improved simulation of aerodynamics, collisions, and human motion; and a Gemini 3.8 Flash agent that plans and checks multi-step events while the world is being rendered. The research preview is rolling out first to trusted testers, followed by selected Google AI Ultra subscribers and early Gemini API and AI Studio partners.

Source: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/)

## OpenAI and Oracle bring enterprise workflows into ChatGPT

Oracle is developing Oracle AI Workspace to connect its applications and governed enterprise data with ChatGPT, letting employees find information and complete approved workflows across areas such as finance, procurement, and planning. The broader partnership also covers an AI Security Mesh built with OpenAI cyber models, a small-business bundle combining ChatGPT Business and Codex with Oracle applications, and an App Composer for building governed internal tools. OpenAI says Oracle already has 130,000 active ChatGPT users and more than 95,000 active Codex users.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/oracle-apps-in-chatgpt/)

## NVIDIA and Meta deepen their agent-infrastructure partnership

NVIDIA and Meta will jointly optimize the Muse model family across training and inference while expanding Meta’s use of Vera Rubin systems, Vera CPUs, Spectrum-X Ethernet, and BlueField-4 networking. They are also considering NVIDIA OpenShell for safer agent deployment and Sentry for behavior monitoring and data processing. The infrastructure agreement is paired with a planned $3 billion NVIDIA investment in Meta’s equity offering, tying agent software, security, networking, and capital more tightly together.

Source: [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-meta-to-expand-collaboration-on-muse-and-muse-family-of-models-amid-robust-growth)

## Starlink Mobile provides emergency connectivity after Spain’s train disaster

Following the deadly train collision near Toledo, Starlink activated free direct-to-device connectivity for compatible Orange and Telefónica phones in affected areas. Customers can use supported apps and text messaging even where terrestrial cell service is unavailable or overloaded. The response is a concrete demonstration of satellite-to-phone service moving beyond planned coverage expansion into emergency communications.

Source: [Starlink](https://x.com/Starlink/status/2115588901196984822)

## Verizon says AI traffic demands a fundamental 6G uplink rethink

Verizon CTO Yago Tenorio argues that 6G cannot be an incremental extension of 5G because AI agents, cameras, wearables, and robots will continuously send contextual data upstream. Those workloads could shift the traditional 8:2 or 9:1 downlink-to-uplink traffic mix toward roughly 1:1. Verizon’s position is that uplink capacity, autonomous network operations, and commercially grounded use cases must shape the architecture and standards process from the beginning.

Source: [RCR Wireless News](https://rcrwireless.com/20261027/carriers/ai-needs-more-than-incremental-6g-fixes-says-verizon-as-uplink-ratio-shifts-11-la28-looms)

## Ukraine advances sovereign satellite communications with Nokia and ICEYE

Ukraine, Nokia, and ICEYE are advancing plans for a secure, sovereign satellite-communications capability. The collaboration combines Nokia’s communications infrastructure with ICEYE’s satellite expertise and is intended to give Ukraine greater control over critical connectivity for defense and national resilience. The effort matters beyond Ukraine: it shows governments treating access to LEO communications as strategic infrastructure rather than a service that can safely depend on a single outside operator.

Source: [Nokia](https://www.nokia.com/newsroom/iceye-and-nokia-partner-with-the-ministry-of-defence-of-ukraine-to-develop-sovereign-satcom-capability/)

## Research Radar

### Martingale-Based Resource Partitioning Bounds for URLLC and eMBB Network Slicing in 5G

Hanbing Li and Yantong Wang derive non-asymptotic bounds for URLLC delay violations and eMBB backlog violations, then use them to find minimum resource-block allocations. At a target violation probability of 10⁻⁶ for each slice, their method requires 44 resource blocks—within a 50-block system—while a conventional effective-capacity baseline requires 64, a reduction of about 31.2%.

Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36602)

### 6G Reasoning Agent Societies: Composition, Scaling, and Consistency-Time Laws

Hatim Chergui, Carolina Fernández-Martínez, Mustapha Benjillali, Mehdi Bennis, and Merouane Debbah model networked LLM agents in a 6G control plane and derive laws for hidden collective divergence and consistency latency. Their results show that topology becomes a control-plane constraint: path networks can exceed a one-second near-real-time budget at 13 agents, while expander structures and radius-three local audits remain bounded.

Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34357)

### Space-Time Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces for Doppler-Resilient LEO Broadcasting

Chandan Kumar Sheemar, Giovanni Iacovelli, Sourabh Solanki, and Symeon Chatzinotas propose time-modulated stacked metasurfaces that assign terminal-specific frequency translations from a single RF chain. In 20 GHz simulations, a four-layer design removed the Doppler floor of a static metasurface for footprint radii above about 45 km and came within 0.5 dB of ideal digital per-terminal precompensation for resolvable users.

Source: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34479)
