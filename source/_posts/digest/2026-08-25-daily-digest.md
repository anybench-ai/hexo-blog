## 🛰️ SpaceX and NVIDIA sketch an orbital AI-compute system

Elon Musk says SpaceX and NVIDIA have designed a space-optimized Vera Rubin NVL72 system for orbital launch in Q4 2027, with larger scale targeted in 2028. If it happens, “AI factory” design moves from datacenter power and cooling into radiation, launch, thermal, and backhaul constraints.

Source: https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/science/spacex-nvidia-develop-space-optimised-ai-system-for-orbital-launch-next-year-elon-musk/article71387191.ece

## 🚀 SpaceX lands a booster for the 37th time after a Starlink launch

SpaceX launched 29 Starlink satellites from Florida this morning and said the Falcon 9 booster completed its 37th launch and landing. That is a fresh launch-cadence and reusability marker, not a repeat of the 100th Falcon-launch story from the weekend.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2092201764179132594

## ⚡ NVIDIA pushes Vera Rubin inference for agent workloads

NVIDIA’s Hot Chips-era update says Groq 3 LPX is in full production as a Vera Rubin NVL72 extension for low-latency token generation in agentic workloads. The useful signal is that vendors are now benchmarking systems around long-running coding and agent sessions, not only chat throughput.

Source: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-rubin-lpx-spectrum-x-nvlink-fusion/

## 📡 Amazon highlights the human pipeline behind Amazon Leo

Amazon profiled hourly employees who used Career Choice to become satellite technicians and then watched the satellites they helped build launch from Cape Canaveral. It is not a technical breakthrough, but it shows Amazon Leo turning from a constellation plan into a manufacturing workforce and launch pipeline.

Source: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/amazon-leo/amazon-career-choice-satellite-technicians-space-launch

## 📱 Qualcomm frames cross-device agentic AI as the user experience

Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon is pitching “an ecosystem of you,” where an AI task follows the user across a Snapdragon PC, phone, and wearable. The strategic point is that edge AI competition is moving toward continuity across devices, not just single-device model demos.

Source: https://x.com/Qualcomm/status/2091922398744359302

## 🧬 Anthropic says Claude designed working protein binders

Anthropic reports that Claude, using a human expert prompt, autonomously designed novel protein binders for 14 of 15 targets, with Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience building and testing the proteins. This is a week-old item but not covered in the last three digests, and it is one of the more concrete AI-for-science results in the source pass.

Source: https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2089842387845804246

## 🧰 GitHub trending keeps tilting toward local agents and reliability tools

The important part for our work is the growing emphasis on reproducibility, memory, and boundaries around agents.

## 📡 Research Radar

### CERTIoT-6G: Continuous Cybersecurity Certification for IoT Devices in 5G/6G Networks

Evangelos Lempesis, Fabio Palmese, Hamed Haddadi, Anna Maria Mandalari. Accepted at IEEE CSCN 2026. The paper proposes a SECaaS framework for automated compliance, live traffic monitoring, and adversarial testing of IoT devices in 5G and future 6G networks.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23339

### Edge-Native Embodied Intelligence for Action-Aware Wireless Edge Networks

Yiru Wang, Chuanao Jiang, Jiahui Cui, Zide Fan, Lei Wang, Zehui Xiong, Dong In Kim. The paper frames 6G edge networks as bidirectional loops where embodied agents use edge cognition and their physical actions feed active federated learning.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17774

### Integrated Sensing and Communications over Hierarchical Cellular and Cell-Free MIMO Systems

Wei Jiang, Hans D. Schotten. IEEE Globecom 2026. The paper studies an ISAC architecture where a central cellular base station senses as a radar while idle distributed APs serve as bistatic receivers.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18873
