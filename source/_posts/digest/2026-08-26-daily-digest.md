## 🔐 ChatGPT Work can now handle signed-in web tasks

ChatGPT said its Work tier can use a computer and browser to sign in to websites on web and mobile without the model seeing the username or password. This is a meaningful agent-product shift because credential handoff is one of the main blockers for real-world task execution.

Source: https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2092366554965107164

## 🍎 Apple refreshes Mac mini around local AI

Apple unveiled a more powerful Mac mini with M6 and M5 Pro options, saying the system delivers up to 4x faster AI performance and is aimed at local model work. The interesting signal is Apple pushing developer-grade AI compute into a tiny desktop rather than treating on-device AI as only a phone feature.

Source: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-unveils-a-more-powerful-mac-mini-featuring-the-all-new-m6-and-m5-pro/

## 🤖 NVIDIA announces Jetson Orin Nano 2 for edge robotics

NVIDIA introduced Jetson Orin Nano 2, an entry-level robotics computer with 78 TOPS, 8GB memory, and roughly 2x the inference performance of Jetson Orin Nano Super. It is a practical edge-AI marker: smaller frontier models are now good enough that NVIDIA is packaging them for drones, robots, and vision systems.

Source: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-jetson-orin-nano-2-robotics-computer-to-redefine-entry-level-edge-ai

## 📡 Research Radar

### A Capability Broker for Workflow-Network QoS Coordination in B5G/6G Industrial Services

Qize Guo, Yan Chen, Taleb Tarik, Bjoern Riemer, Hemant Zope, Hao Yu. The paper proposes a software control-plane broker that tracks lifecycle-managed QoS commitments for multi-phase industrial workflows in B5G/6G networks, including duplicate-safe admission, event ordering, and recovery routing.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24496

### Coherent Direct D-MIMO Localization

Benjamin J. B. Deutschmann, Lukas D'Angelo, Erik Leitinger, Klaus Witrisal. The paper builds Bayesian filtering methods for coherent near-field distributed MIMO localization, with particle-based belief propagation that parallelizes across particles and antenna panels and can run in tens of milliseconds on GPU.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24880

### ORBITALIF: An Efficient Spiking Federated Learning Framework for Onboard Cloud Removal

Bohan Zhang, Chenyu Xu, Yijie Mao, Yuanming Shi. Accepted by IEEE GLOBECOM 2026. The paper uses spiking federated learning across LEO satellites to remove clouds onboard while cutting neuromorphic inference energy by 98.6% versus an equivalent ANN.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24073
