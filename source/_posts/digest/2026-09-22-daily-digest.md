Today’s strongest signal is that next-generation networking is becoming operational across borders and new spectrum. T-Mobile and Jio demonstrated native 5G Standalone roaming, while the UAE is moving upper 6 GHz toward commercial service. In AI, attention is spreading from raw model capability to mathematical governance, edge hardware, agent infrastructure, and the energy systems needed to sustain deployment.

## T-Mobile and Jio complete the first 5G Standalone roaming connection

T-Mobile and Reliance Jio say they have completed the world’s first 5G Standalone roaming connection. Syniverse provided the interoperability layer between the U.S. and Indian operators’ SA networks.

The technical importance is continuity: international roamers can remain on cloud-native 5G cores rather than falling back to non-standalone or legacy architectures. That creates a cleaner path for advanced 5G capabilities—including lower latency, slicing, and policy-aware services—to travel across operator boundaries.

Source: [T-Mobile](https://investor.t-mobile.com/news-releases/news-release-details/t-mobile-and-jio-achieve-worlds-first-5g-standalone-roaming)

## UAE moves upper 6 GHz toward commercial service

The UAE is advancing upper-6-GHz spectrum into commercial deployment after engineering work by the national regulator and mobile operators. Reported results indicate the band can provide roughly three times the capacity of C-band at comparable coverage while reusing existing sites.

The n104 band is also expected to enter national device-approval requirements, with compatible commercial devices arriving from September 2026. The combination of wide channels and more favorable propagation than higher FR3 spectrum makes upper 6 GHz a practical bridge from 5G-Advanced into 6G.

Source: [SatellitePro Middle East](https://satelliteprome.com/news/uae-advances-upper-6-ghz-spectrum-into-commercial-deployment/)

## OpenAI creates an independent mathematics advisory group

OpenAI is forming an independent group of mathematicians to advise how the company assesses and communicates AI-generated mathematical advances. The group will also address academic and professional standards and help shape tools for mathematical research and learning.

This is a useful governance move because mathematical claims are unusually easy to state confidently and unusually difficult to verify at the research frontier. External disciplinary judgment can help separate genuine advances from plausible-looking but incorrect derivations.

Source: [OpenAI on X](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2102093145051943229)

## xAI releases Grok 4.7

xAI released Grok 4.7, positioning it as a stronger model for coding and knowledge work. NVIDIA confirmed that the model is supported by its accelerated-computing platform, while Elon Musk emphasized the combination of intelligence, speed, and lower cost.

The release continues the rapid compression of frontier-model cycles: vendors are now competing not only on benchmark capability but on the latency and economics needed to keep agents continuously active.

Source: [NVIDIA on X](https://x.com/nvidia/status/2102081129234751722)

## Qualcomm’s Snapdragon X powers the new Googlebook

Qualcomm announced that Snapdragon X-series silicon powers the new Googlebook, an Android-based laptop designed around Gemini Intelligence. The product combines Google’s assistant layer with an Arm PC-class processor and native Android ecosystem.

The announcement expands on-device AI competition beyond phones and conventional Windows laptops. It also gives Google a first-party form factor in which Gemini features and local inference can be designed together from the start.

Source: [Qualcomm on X](https://x.com/Qualcomm/status/2102195832871391471)

## NVIDIA applies AI and digital twins to lower-carbon power systems

NVIDIA highlighted five companies applying its AI, accelerated simulation, and digital-twin technologies to power systems. The projects span advanced nuclear plants, fusion experiments, grid planning, and reuse of electric-vehicle batteries.

Energy is becoming a binding constraint on AI infrastructure, so this work is strategically relevant beyond climate technology. Better plant simulation and grid optimization could shorten deployment cycles and improve utilization as data-center electricity demand rises.

Source: [NVIDIA on X](https://x.com/nvidia/status/2102159628020666805)

## Cua’s computer-use agent stack surges on GitHub

The open-source Cua project is trending with a broad “computer-use 2.0” stack: cross-platform desktop drivers, isolated cloud desktops, local macOS and Linux virtual machines, specialist decision models, and evaluation benchmarks.

Its architecture reflects a maturing agent ecosystem. Instead of treating visual control as one monolithic model capability, Cua separates planning, bounded interface decisions, execution, isolation, and evaluation—components that can be tested and replaced independently.

Source: [trycua/cua on GitHub](https://github.com/trycua/cua)

## U.S. and China set a follow-up AI-safety meeting

Senior U.S. and Chinese officials plan to meet again in roughly two months in Shenzhen to discuss AI dangers and communication protocols for serious incidents. This follows the U.S. proposal for a bilateral notification channel during the current round of talks.

The scheduled follow-up is a genuine step beyond the initial proposal: it creates a continuing process for risk-reduction dialogue even while export controls and semiconductor policy remain contested separately.

Source: [Reuters](https://reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-china-meet-again-ai-safety-two-months-shenzhen-bessent-says-2026-09-21)

## Research Radar

### Explanation-Guided Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for Joint Resource Allocation and Scheduling in 6G in-X Subnetworks

Ramoni Adeogun combines multi-agent reinforcement learning, federated learning, and explainable AI to coordinate channel allocation and device scheduling across dense industrial subnetworks without sharing raw local data. Simulations based on 3GPP scenarios report improved reliability, robustness, fairness, and transparency over existing approaches. The paper is accepted at IEEE VTC Fall 2026.

Source: [arXiv:2609.24102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24102)

### 5G-Shark: A Network Security Auditor for 5G Subscriber Privacy and Unauthenticated Signalling Resilience

Oscar Lasierra, Gines Garcia-Aviles, Antonio Skarmeta, and Xavier Costa-Pérez present an open-source SDR-based auditing method tested against commercial 5G Standalone networks. It separates specification-level weaknesses from implementation failures while demonstrating identifier exposure, forced downgrade, denial-of-service states, and linkable temporary identifiers that can enable persistent tracking.

Source: [arXiv:2609.24656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24656)

### Joint Energy Efficiency and Fairness Optimization for D2D Communications in Aerial-Ground Integrated Heterogeneous Networks

Chuan-Chi Lai, Ang-Hsun Tsai, and Shang-Long Wu propose a multi-channel shared-reuse algorithm for D2D links in networks combining terrestrial macrocells and UAV base stations. Compared with exclusive reuse, simulations report about 397% higher D2D energy efficiency and 542% higher throughput; the paper is accepted by IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology.

Source: [arXiv:2609.24365](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24365)
