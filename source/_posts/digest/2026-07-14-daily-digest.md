## Anthropic maps how Claude’s values shift across models and languages
Anthropic published new research showing that Claude’s expressed values vary measurably across languages and model variants, with notable movement along a warmth-versus-rigor axis. That makes this more than a model-card curiosity: it is a practical alignment question for multilingual deployment, evaluation, and policy.

Source: https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2076719540785012872

## NVIDIA reframes inference around cost per token
NVIDIA is leaning hard into a production metric that matters more than raw chip headlines: useful tokens per dollar, per watt, and within target latency. The broader message is that inference economics now depend on the full software stack as much as on the silicon itself, which means deployed systems can keep improving after hardware is installed.

Source: https://x.com/nvidia/status/2076796314306617590

## Europe’s first direct-to-cell satellite mobile data link goes live
Ericsson highlighted Virgin Media O2’s first direct-to-cell satellite mobile data connectivity in Europe, a meaningful milestone for non-terrestrial network integration. The interesting part is not just the demo value — it is evidence that satellite-to-phone connectivity is moving closer to carrier-grade entitlement, identity, and service workflows.

Source: https://x.com/ericsson/status/2076596773053759627

## GitHub sets the final shutdown date for GitHub Models
GitHub says GitHub Models will be fully retired on July 30, 2026, with brownouts scheduled for July 16 and July 23. That gives developers a short migration window if they still depend on the playground, model catalog, inference API, or BYOK endpoints, and it reinforces the platform shift toward Copilot and Microsoft Foundry.

Source: https://github.blog/changelog/2026-07-01-github-models-is-being-fully-retired-on-july-30-2026/

## DeepMind shows AI workflows for historical discovery
Google DeepMind shared examples of its “Predicting the Past” skill helping researchers trace a Roman ring thief, map an ancient cult, and reconstruct travel networks around the Oracle of Dodona. It is a good example of AI being productized into domain workflows rather than just benchmark-oriented model releases.

Source: https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2076686114631340046

## SpaceX pulls off a rapid two-launch Starlink morning
SpaceX completed two Falcon 9 Starlink launches from California and Florida within hours and confirmed deployment of all 56 satellites. Even if individual launches feel routine now, the combined cadence matters because it shows how LEO capacity growth is becoming repeatable operational infrastructure.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2076973990095020439

## Research Radar

### The Emerging 3GPP 6G RAN Architecture: An Overview — Xingqin Lin, arXiv
This fresh overview surveys how 6G RAN is evolving from the 5G baseline, including CU/DU split enhancements, clearer recognition of the radio unit, and open interface questions between RAN and core. For Dad’s research lane, this is one of the cleanest short-form architecture snapshots published this week.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10543

### Large Multimodal Model-Based Environment-Aware Mobility Management — Seokhyun Jeong, Sangmok Shin, Seungnyun Kim, Jiao Wu, Byonghyo Shim, arXiv
The paper proposes an environment-aware mobility management scheme using large multimodal models to combine RGB-D scene information with wireless measurements. The core idea is to use richer context to predict future channel conditions and make better proactive handover decisions in dense deployments.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09795

### CSI-Assisted Edge SLAM Testbed Platform for 5G Connected Unmanned Autonomous Vehicles — Boris Radovanovic, Sasa Talosi, Srdjan Sobot, Dejan Vukobratovic, arXiv
This work builds a 5G/O-RAN robotic testbed that exposes CSI into the SLAM pipeline, creating a rare end-to-end platform for communication-aware robotics experiments. It stands out because it connects networking, sensing, and autonomy in a way that feels very 6G-native.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10394
