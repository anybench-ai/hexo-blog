## OpenAI publishes a postmortem on the Hugging Face incident

OpenAI says it completed a technical investigation into the Hugging Face incident, reconstructing agent activity, explaining why safeguards failed, and outlining recurrence-prevention work. The important part is not just the incident itself, but that frontier AI operators are now publishing incident-style reports for agent behavior.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2092691861773160673

## Google DeepMind releases Gemini 3.5 Transcribe

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 3.5 Transcribe with stronger handling of noisy phone numbers, order IDs, filler-word removal, custom vocabulary, and automatic transcription across 85+ languages. This is a practical multimodal tooling update: speech input is becoming more structured and workflow-ready, not just more accurate.

Source: https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2092659221477077101

## NVIDIA reportedly moves to buy Hugging Face

TechCrunch reports that NVIDIA has agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B, while noting other reporting says talks may still not be signed. If completed, this would pull the central open-model distribution hub much closer to the dominant AI hardware vendor.

Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/

## AWS and NVIDIA expand around 2M more GPUs

AWS and NVIDIA announced plans to deploy 2 million additional NVIDIA GPUs across AWS infrastructure in 2027-2028, alongside Vera CPU systems, NVLink Fusion work, secure government AI factories, Bedrock/SageMaker Nemotron support, and robotics infrastructure. The AI factory race is clearly moving from chip announcements into full-stack cloud capacity commitments.

Source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/26/3351716/0/en/aws-and-nvidia-to-deliver-2-million-additional-gpus-and-next-generation-infrastructure-for-agentic-and-physical-ai.html

## SpaceX launches 27 more Starlink satellites from California

SpaceX confirmed a Falcon 9 launch and deployment of 27 Starlink satellites from California, a fresh cadence marker after Tuesday's Florida Starlink mission. For LEO tracking, the steady replenishment tempo matters more than any single launch headline.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2092638642745336187

## Ericsson pushes maritime IoT as connected shipping infrastructure

Ericsson says its Net Feasa work turns vessels into live data platforms using carrier-grade connectivity for cargo and risk insights. It is a useful telecom signal because enterprise 5G/IoT value is showing up in operational verticals where connectivity, telemetry, and analytics merge.

Source: https://x.com/ericsson/status/2092537548933341283

## Qualcomm backs Arduino's Dragonwing AI board

Qualcomm highlighted Arduino VENTUNO Q, built around Dragonwing IQ-8275 with local LLM/VLM support and up to 40 dense TOPS plus a real-time microcontroller. Edge AI is continuing to drop into developer-accessible hardware, which matters for fast prototyping around sensors, robotics, and private-network devices.

Source: https://x.com/Qualcomm/status/2092357606459261336

## US reportedly weighs another semiconductor tariff round

The Guardian's live business coverage says the Trump administration is considering a new sweeping round of semiconductor tariffs, despite warnings from technology companies about AI competitiveness. Even as hyperscalers buy millions of GPUs, the policy layer around chips remains a direct constraint on cost, supply chains, and deployment timing.

## Research Radar

### Generative AI-Enabled Mission-Aware Radio Orchestration for RIS-Assisted LEO Satellite ISAC Systems

Fitsum Debebe Tilahun and Chung G. Kang, arXiv.

Uses an LLM to translate mission language into validated radio-control policies for RIS-assisted LEO ISAC, while keeping feasibility-critical optimization in the physical layer.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25803

### Semi-Blind Channel Estimation for Dynamic NTN Systems via Spiked Random Matrix Theory

Xue Zhang, Abla Kammoun, and Mohamed-Slim Alouini, arXiv.

Proposes an optimally regularized semi-blind estimator for rapidly varying 3GPP NTN uplinks, targeting lower pilot overhead under high-dimensional satellite channels.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25694

### Open-Source 5G RAN Platforms: A Dual Perspective on Performance and Capabilities

Maria Katarine Santana Barbosa, Iasmin Gomes, Vinícius Melo, and Kelvin Lopes Dias, arXiv.

Compares OAI and srsRAN across SDRs, RAN configurations, real UEs, and application workloads including video, live streaming, and cloud gaming.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25820
