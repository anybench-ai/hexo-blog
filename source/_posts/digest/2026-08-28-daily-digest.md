## OpenAI and 100+ organizations call for a cyber-defense surge

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, AWS, Oracle, and others signed an open letter urging governments and companies to use the current window to harden critical infrastructure against AI-amplified attacks. This is a new development after yesterday's OpenAI incident report: the focus has shifted from postmortem to sector-wide mobilization.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2093074192636018977

## Anthropic previews a Model Hardware Standard for physical agents

Anthropic opened a research preview of MHS, a shared interface for AI agents to safely operate lab and manufacturing equipment. Early examples include drug-discovery experiments, compressed imaging workflows, and quantum-computer laser stabilization, which makes this one of the clearer bridges from software agents into controlled physical systems.

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview

## Google rolls out Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash for controllable AI video

Google announced Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash for video generation and editing, adding scene extension, start/end frame control, image-to-video, and higher-resolution output paths. The useful signal is that video models are becoming editable production tools rather than one-shot generators.

Source: https://x.com/Google/status/2093008576487072064

## Starlink Mobile goes live in Costa Rica with Liberty

Starlink says Liberty customers in Costa Rica can now use Starlink Mobile for satellite connectivity on phones in remote areas, including voice notes, location sharing, and maps. This is a direct-to-cell deployment signal in Latin America, where coverage extension is often the highest-value first use case.

Source: https://x.com/Starlink/status/2093069805129744537

## Z.ai pushes a low-cost model running on Chinese chips

CNBC reports that Z.ai released GLM-5.3-Flash, a low-cost model the company says runs entirely on homegrown Chinese semiconductors. The model story matters less than the infrastructure story: Chinese labs are optimizing models around domestic chip constraints, which can reshape cost and deployment assumptions even when peak capability trails frontier systems.

Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/zai-shares-surge-new-ai-model-using-chinese-chips.html

## China frames AI, chips, robotics, and 6G as its next industrial stack

DigiTimes reports that China is setting a five-year industrial strategy centered on AI, semiconductors, 6G, robotics, and advanced manufacturing. For wireless research, the 6G mention is part of a larger national production strategy, not just a telecom roadmap.

Source: https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260827VL228/china-6g-ai-robotics-chips-industrial-manufacturing.html

## GitHub spotlights OpenClaw's maintainer ecosystem

GitHub published a maintainer roundtable on OpenClaw's growth, describing a project that reached roughly 388,000 stars, 81,000 forks, and 80,000+ commits by late August. The agent-tooling signal is that governance, security, and maintainer workflows are becoming as important as raw agent features.

Source: https://github.blog/open-source/maintainers/openclaw-went-viral-meet-the-maintainers-building-and-securing-it/

## Research Radar

### Multi-UE Networked Sensing: A New Paradigm for 6G Perceptive Mobile Networks

J. Andrew Zhang et al., arXiv. The paper introduces multi-user-equipment sensing for 6G ISAC, including uplink, downlink, hybrid architectures, and fusion challenges across correlated UE observations.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25597

### Rethinking the Foundations of Two-Sided AI Models for 6G

Yongjeong Oh et al., arXiv. The paper revisits transmitter/receiver AI models with practical deployment constraints: 5G NR coexistence, compact model tables, and gradient-free scalar-feedback adaptation.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22918

### AirMoE: Realizing Over-the-Air Distributed Mixture-of-Experts Inference at the Wireless Edge

Huiling Yang et al., arXiv. The paper uses over-the-air computation to aggregate distributed MoE expert outputs at the wireless edge, reducing uplink overhead under heterogeneous channels.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22932
