# Morning Digest — Monday, June 22, 2026

## 1) Starlink is connecting 30,000+ students across 160 schools in Peru
Starlink said it is partnering with Enseña Perú and BCP to provide reliable connectivity to underserved schools across Peru. This matters because LEO connectivity keeps showing up not just as premium broadband, but as public-service infrastructure for education and inclusion.

Source: https://x.com/Starlink/status/2068771628344066495

## 2) SpaceX launched 24 more Starlink satellites from California
SpaceX completed another Falcon 9 Starlink mission from California on June 21, 2026, and later confirmed deployment of all 24 satellites. The deeper pattern is operational: reusable launch cadence is becoming one of the most important strategic moats in the LEO market.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2068763409940705752

## 3) Google DeepMind is testing an AI planning assistant with UK councils
Google DeepMind said its Gemini-based planning prototype is being co-developed with the UK government, Google Cloud, Faculty, and local authorities in Barnet, Camden, and Dorset. The stated goal is to cut householder planning-application decision times by 50%, which makes this one of the cleaner examples of frontier AI entering high-friction government paperwork workflows.

Source: https://deepmind.google/blog/unlocking-uk-house-building-with-ai-accelerated-planning/

## 4) GitHub launched agent finder for Copilot
GitHub announced that Copilot can now discover MCP servers, skills, canvases, tools, and agents on demand through an indexed registry instead of requiring everything to be manually pre-wired. That is a meaningful architectural shift: agents become more modular, leaner in context, and easier to govern at the enterprise boundary.

Source: https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-17-agent-finder-for-github-copilot-now-available/

## 5) Anthropic says a U.S. directive forced it to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5
Anthropic published a statement saying a U.S. government export-control directive required it to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for all customers. Anthropic argues the cited jailbreak-related concerns were narrow and not unique, but the broader implication is clear: access to frontier models can now be constrained directly by national-security policy, not just company policy.

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access

## 6) Coherent secured a proposed $50M CHIPS award tied to AI photonics expansion
Coherent announced a proposed CHIPS and Science Act award of up to $50 million to expand its Sherman, Texas indium-phosphide photonics facility. The company says the expansion will double manufacturing space and quadruple wafer capacity for optical networking components used in AI datacenters, with NVIDIA highlighted as a major partner.

Source: https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/a-chip-letter-of-intent-for-50m-to-expand-world-leading-manufacturing-facility-for-ai-infrastructure

## 7) NVIDIA posted fresh MLPerf Training 6.0 wins at Blackwell scale
NVIDIA said Blackwell led every MLPerf Training v6.0 benchmark and scaled to 8,192 GPUs in production cloud environments, including a 7.07-minute Llama 3.1 405B result. The interesting part is not just the benchmark win, but how much the gains now depend on software, routing, memory behavior, and full-stack communications optimization.

Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-blackwell-tops-mlperf-training-6-0-with-industry-leading-scale-and-performance/

## Research Radar

### Max-Min Rate Fairness Optimization for Multi-User Pinching-Antenna NOMA Systems
**Authors:** Mahmoud AlaaEldin, Amy Inwood, Xidong Mu, Michail Matthaiou  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper studies how to jointly optimize pinching-antenna placement and precoding in a multi-user NOMA downlink. It is interesting less because pinching antennas are mainstream today, and more because it pushes an unusual 6G hardware concept toward a concrete systems optimization problem.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20450v1

### Hybrid TRP-UE Sensing for Enhanced Target Localization
**Authors:** Necati Kagan Erkek, Marco Di Renzo, Arman Shojaeifard, Yasser Mestrah, Remun Koirala, Mohammad Heggo  
**Venue:** arXiv  
The authors propose combining transmitter-point sensing with UE-assisted sensing under 3GPP-compliant ISAC channel models. That makes it directly relevant to 6G integrated sensing-and-communications work where localization performance matters in difficult environments.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20413v1

### Data-Aided Channel and Doppler Estimation for mMIMO LEO SatComs with Uncompensated Doppler
**Authors:** Abdollah Masoud Darya, Saeed Abdallah  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper tackles the practical challenge of channel and Doppler estimation in massive-MIMO LEO satellite communications. It stands out because uncompensated Doppler is one of the real pain points that separates LEO networking from ordinary terrestrial wireless assumptions.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16750v1
