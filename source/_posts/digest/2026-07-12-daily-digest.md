## Top Stories

### 1) OpenAI is positioning GPT-5.6 as a cheaper, stronger model for health intelligence
OpenAI says GPT-5.6 improves health-intelligence performance while lowering cost across the lineup, with Luna reportedly outperforming GPT-5.5’s highest reasoning setting at much lower cost. The strategic signal is that OpenAI is pushing frontier-model gains into domain-specific utility where procurement and deployment decisions actually get made.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2075686461693898868

### 2) OpenAI says GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra produced a proof of a 50-year-old theorem
OpenAI highlighted a proof result produced with GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra. If the claim withstands scrutiny, it adds to the case that recent progress is showing up in long-horizon, high-structure reasoning tasks rather than only consumer-facing polish.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2075644390689898690

### 5) NVIDIA says a DGX GB300 system will go live at NPS Monterey this month

### 6) SpaceX is targeting July 16 for Starship’s thirteenth flight test
SpaceX says Starship Flight 13 could launch as early as Wednesday, July 16. The program’s cadence remains important because Starship is still one of the clearest leading indicators for long-term launch-cost compression and the feasibility of more ambitious orbital infrastructure.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2076075162420629714

### 7) Starlink has begun flying on Copa Airlines
Starlink says connectivity is now live on Copa Airlines, with the first equipped aircraft already having completed its inaugural flight. This is a nice, concrete data point for the LEO broadband story expanding into commercial aviation in a more visible way.

Source: https://x.com/Starlink/status/2074484417238868017

## Research Radar

### HAPS as a Hypercell: Enabling Coverage and Capacity Carrier Shutdown in Cellular Networks
**Authors:** Matteo Bernabè, David López-Pérez, Nicola Piovesan  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper looks at whether high-altitude platform systems can preserve service while allowing terrestrial networks to shut carriers down more aggressively for energy savings. It is especially interesting as operators start treating energy efficiency as a design constraint rather than just an operational cost.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06072v1

### Handover-Optimal User Association Policy for LEO Satellite-based 5G NTN
**Authors:** Pradnya Taksande, Jainesh Mehta, Prasanna Chaporkar  
**Venue:** arXiv  
A strong fit for Bruski’s research lane. The paper focuses on user association in LEO-based 5G non-terrestrial networks, where frequent handovers can dominate user experience and control overhead.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04829v1

### A Simultaneous Clustering and Tracking Algorithm for Capturing Cluster-Level Spatial Consistency in 6G Wireless Channels
**Authors:** Jiaxin Lin, Pan Tang, Jianhua Zhang, Zhaowei Chang, Peijie Liu  
**Venue:** arXiv  
A good channel-modeling pick for 6G. The contribution is about preserving cluster-level spatial consistency, which matters if future wireless simulations are supposed to capture realistic motion and propagation evolution instead of frozen snapshots.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04664v1
