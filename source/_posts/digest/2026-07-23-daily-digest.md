# Morning Digest for Thursday, July 23, 2026

## 1) OpenAI launches Presence for enterprise voice and chat agents
OpenAI introduced **Presence**, a limited-general-availability deployment product for enterprises that want AI agents to handle customer and internal workflows with explicit policies, guardrails, approved actions, and escalation paths. The bigger strategic point is that OpenAI is no longer just selling raw model capability here; it is selling a full operational layer for governed, production-grade agents.

Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/

## 2) OpenAI backs the U.S. Genesis Mission for scientific R&D
OpenAI said on **July 22, 2026** that it is working with the U.S. government, National Laboratories, universities, and researchers to support the **Genesis Mission**, an effort to pair frontier AI with federal scientific infrastructure. For research workflows, this matters because it frames AI as part of a national scientific productivity stack rather than just a commercial assistant layer.

Source: https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-next-era-of-national-science/

## 3) Cisco releases Antares, open-weight security SLMs for vulnerability localization
Cisco unveiled **Antares-350M** and **Antares-1B**, compact open-weight security models built to help defenders localize known vulnerabilities within large repositories. The interesting angle is economic as much as technical: Cisco is arguing that meaningful software-security triage can increasingly be done with smaller, locally runnable models instead of expensive frontier-scale inference.

Source: https://blogs.cisco.com/ai/introducing-antares-the-most-efficient-open-weight-ai-models-for-vulnerability-localization

## 4) NVIDIA brings Spectrum-6 into gigascale AI factories
NVIDIA says its **102.4-terabit-per-second Spectrum-6 Ethernet switch system** is now arriving across major AI-factory deployments as part of the Vera Rubin platform. That is noteworthy because it underlines a now-familiar constraint in frontier AI: once GPU clusters reach gigascale, networking becomes a first-class compute bottleneck and a major differentiator for token throughput.

Source: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-spectrum-six-arrives-in-gigascale-ai-factories/

## 5) Nokia pushes AI-RAN from concept toward commercial rollout
Nokia says its new **AI-native RAN platform**, built with NVIDIA Aerial AI-RAN, is designed to deliver **more than 100% spectral-efficiency gains by 2028** while keeping operators on a software-defined path toward 6G. For wireless researchers, this is one of the clearest recent signs that AI-RAN is being positioned as an actual commercial architecture with upgrade logic and economic claims, not just a conference narrative.

Source: https://www.nokia.com/newsroom/nokia-defines-the-next-era-of-radio-with-the-industrys-first-ai-native-ran-platform/

## 6) Amazon Leo signs a South Africa satellite-internet deal with Herotel
Amazon Leo said **Herotel**, South Africa’s largest fixed internet provider, will use its LEO network to launch **evry**, a satellite-internet service aimed at underserved rural areas. The main takeaway is that the LEO race is increasingly about market-entry partnerships and field deployment models, not merely constellation scale or launch cadence.

Source: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/amazon-leo/amazon-leo-south-africa-herotel-evry

## Research Radar

### JEPA-CFM: A Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture-based Channel Foundation Model for Robust Fluid Antenna Systems
A very fresh arXiv paper that pushes toward channel foundation models for fluid antenna systems, making it especially relevant to 6G-oriented physical-layer research.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20202v1

### Intelligent Multi-UAV Navigation in ITNTNs: A Hierarchical LLM Approach
This paper explores hierarchical LLM control for multi-UAV navigation inside integrated terrestrial/non-terrestrial networks, which is right on the boundary between network intelligence and embodied autonomy.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18604v1

### Profiling and Scheduling Complex O-RAN Applications Across the 5G Edge and Cloud
A practical systems paper on how to place and schedule O-RAN applications across heterogeneous edge and cloud resources, with clear relevance to AI-native radio deployments.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12230v1
