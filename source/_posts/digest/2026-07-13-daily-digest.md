## Morning Theme

Today’s pattern is productization at every layer: voice AI, controllable model safety, creative-model APIs, on-device inference, Open RAN interoperability, and LEO deployment are all getting more operational.

## Top Stories

### 1) OpenAI is rolling out GPT-Live globally inside ChatGPT Voice
OpenAI’s new GPT-Live system brings full-duplex voice interaction to ChatGPT, allowing more natural turn-taking, interruptions, and conversational flow. The more important strategic signal is that OpenAI is treating voice as a serious interface for longer, higher-value workflows rather than a demo feature.

Source: [https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live/](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live/)

### 2) Anthropic is testing an “off switch” for dual-use model knowledge
Anthropic and AE Studio published research on GRAM, a training method intended to place dual-use capabilities into removable modules. If this line of work holds up, it suggests a more fine-grained safety direction: isolating dangerous capabilities without discarding the rest of a model’s usefulness.

Source: [https://www.anthropic.com/research/off-switch-dual-use](https://www.anthropic.com/research/off-switch-dual-use)

### 3) Runway launched Runway Dev for API-first AI media products
Runway Dev packages the company’s image, video, audio, and real-time character capabilities into a developer-facing platform with enterprise controls. That matters because the creative-AI market is shifting from consumer-facing generation tools toward API infrastructure that other products can build on.

Source: [https://runwayml.com/news/introducing-runway-dev](https://runwayml.com/news/introducing-runway-dev)

### 4) Qualcomm is pushing GenieX as an open runtime for on-device GenAI
Qualcomm’s GenieX developer preview is meant to simplify local deployment of generative models across Windows, Android, and Linux environments on Qualcomm hardware. For edge AI and mobile inference, this is a practical step toward easier shipping and optimization instead of fragmented device-specific integration work.

Source: [https://www.qualcomm.com/developer/blog/2026/06/geniex-developer-preview](https://www.qualcomm.com/developer/blog/2026/06/geniex-developer-preview)

### 5) O-RAN’s Spring 2026 PlugFest showed deeper massive-MIMO and AI-RAN integration
According to O-RAN ALLIANCE, the Spring 2026 Global PlugFest spanned 9 labs and 31 participants, with progress in massive-MIMO interoperability, energy-efficiency work, and expanded open-source software usage. For wireless systems research, this is a meaningful sign that Open RAN collaboration is still moving from testbed validation toward operational maturity.

Source: [https://www.o-ran.org/news-and-events](https://www.o-ran.org/news-and-events)

### 6) SpaceX is targeting another 24-satellite Starlink launch tonight
SpaceX’s next Falcon 9 mission from California is scheduled to deliver 24 more Starlink satellites into low-Earth orbit. Even when routine, these launches matter because constellation scale, replacement cadence, and service quality all increasingly depend on launch tempo.

Source: [https://www.spacex.com/launches/sl-17-45](https://www.spacex.com/launches/sl-17-45)

## Research Radar

### Multi-Domain Iterative Detection for Massive Connectivity in LEO Satellite Networks
**Authors:** Xinhua Liu, Yueqing Wang, Keke Ying, Peihu Duan, Dapeng Li  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper targets dense access and detection problems in LEO systems, making it relevant as constellations move toward heavier concurrency and broader service layers.

🔗 [http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09212v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09212v1)

### ORAN-DEFEND: Subspace Detection and Sanitization of Backdoor DRL xApps in Open RAN
**Authors:** Md Raihan Uddin, Fatemeh Lotfi, Tolunay Seyfi, Fatemeh Afghah  
**Venue:** arXiv  
A timely paper on securing AI-driven xApps against backdoors, which becomes increasingly important as Open RAN control intelligence moves closer to production workflows.

🔗 [http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06647v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06647v1)

### 6G Sensing Security: Distributed Game-Theoretic RL for Urban Beamforming and Attacker Detection
**Authors:** Parmida Geranmayeh, Onur Günlü  
**Venue:** arXiv  
A strong cross-domain contribution linking 6G sensing, urban beamforming, reinforcement learning, and adversarial defense.

🔗 [http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06115v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06115v1)
