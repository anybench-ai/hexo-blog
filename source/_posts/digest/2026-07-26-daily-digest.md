## OpenAI says it will publish a technical report on the Hugging Face incident

OpenAI said speculation is still circulating around the Hugging Face security incident and that it is continuing a formal review with external advisors and oversight from its Safety & Security Committee. The interesting part is not just the incident itself, but the normalization of post-incident disclosure and governance language around frontier-model misuse.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2080815626113954288

## Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, a lightweight cyber-focused model designed to help security teams surface and patch vulnerabilities. It is starting as a limited-access pilot for governments and trusted partners, which suggests Google sees cyber models as controlled infrastructure, not mass-market toys.

Source: https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2080321516814647630

## SpaceX launches another 24 Starlink satellites from California

SpaceX confirmed a Falcon 9 launch carrying 24 Starlink satellites from California. Coming immediately after the latest Starship test cycle, it reinforces the practical reality of the LEO market: repeated launch execution and satellite deployment cadence remain the core moat.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2081078956535488770

## Starlink service reaches Ascension Island

Starlink announced that service is now live on Ascension Island. This is the kind of incremental expansion that rarely dominates headlines but steadily strengthens the constellation’s real-world utility in remote and strategically awkward geographies.

Source: https://x.com/Starlink/status/2080816831317897396

## Ericsson and LG Uplus push AI voice deeper into the network

Ericsson said it is working with LG Uplus to make AI voice services more accessible by embedding intelligence into the network instead of tying it to particular apps or devices. If that architecture holds up, carrier-side AI voice could become a more reusable service layer for telecom operators.

Source: https://x.com/ericsson/status/2080307186438127893

## Qualcomm leans into Samsung’s new on-device AI push

Qualcomm highlighted that Snapdragon now powers AI experiences across Samsung’s newest foldables, watches, and smart eyewear announced around Galaxy Unpacked. The bigger pattern is ecosystem-level AI differentiation: compute, models, and experiences are being coordinated across multiple device classes at once.

Source: https://x.com/Qualcomm/status/2080775128024072420

## Research Radar

### Empowering Rural Areas with Multi-radio Microwave Backhaul Supported by Digital Twin for 5G IAB-based FWA

**Authors:** Anselme Ndikumana, Kim Khoa Nguyen, Adel Larabi, Mohamed Cheriet  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper combines microwave backhaul, 5G integrated access and backhaul, and a digital twin framework to improve rural fixed-wireless coverage while reducing energy use.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21310

### Update the Unseen Only: Minimizing AoI for Collaborative Perception through Online Learning

**Authors:** Yanan Ma, Yuyuan Chang, Zhaorui Wang, Quan Zhou, He Chen, Yong Zhou, R. Srikant, Ness B. Shroff  
**Venue:** arXiv  
A good fit for connected-vehicle networking work: it tackles stale shared sensing data with an online-learning scheduler that explicitly accounts for local sensing.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20967

### Efficient MoE Routing on Direct-Connect Topologies

**Authors:** Ori Cohen, Moran Feldman, Ceren Gazi, Haim Kaplan, Kevin T. Kelly, Eviatar Malkamaki, Alexander Moshkovitz, Keshav N. Muralidhara, K. V. Rashmi, Yonatan Yacoby  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This one is more AI-infrastructure than radio, but still relevant: it shows how better routing strategy can reduce sparse expert-traffic bottlenecks in large mixture-of-experts systems.

🔗 https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20220
