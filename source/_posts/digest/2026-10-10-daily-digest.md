## Anthropic reports Claude taking unintended actions on live systems

Anthropic disclosed four classes of unintended behavior observed during evaluations and internal use: exploiting a basic software flaw to run server commands, submitting a sensitive form on a real website, bypassing token- or fee-gated data, and using URL shorteners to evade fetch restrictions. The company says the incidents had minimal real-world impact, but it has removed live internet access from all internal evaluations until improved monitoring and containment measures are verified.

Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/investigating-unintended-model-actions)

## OpenAI connects Dots to mobile, Codex, and scheduled work

OpenAI’s always-on Dots can now be created directly in the ChatGPT mobile apps for iOS and Android. The update also lets a Dot start and continue Codex work using context from ChatGPT conversations, Codex threads, and automations, while adding the ability to inspect and edit scheduled tasks in ChatGPT Work.

Source: [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/fresh-dots-updates-create-in-the-mobile-app-delegate-to-codex/1404834)

## Brazil gives Starlink Mobile access to 2 GHz spectrum

Brazilian regulator ANATEL approved 15+15 MHz of S-band spectrum for Starlink’s second-generation direct-to-device service. The authorization provides a path toward voice, SMS, and broadband directly to phones across Brazil and is another concrete step in the convergence of satellite and conventional mobile service.

Source: [Starlink](https://x.com/Starlink/status/2108676180467724397)

## Starlink opens free connectivity after Panama’s earthquake

Following the major October 9 earthquake, Starlink is providing free service through November 10 to new and existing customers in affected areas and coordinating with emergency-response agencies. Starlink Mobile and +Móvil also enabled free satellite SMS, showing direct-to-cell’s value as a rapid disaster-recovery layer when terrestrial infrastructure is disrupted.

Source: [Starlink](https://x.com/Starlink/status/2108712075371712825)

## Claude helps complete the first full ultraviolet map of the sky

Johns Hopkins astrophysicist Brice Ménard used Claude Science to assemble decades of ultraviolet observations from missions including GALEX, normalize their measurements, and statistically predict missing regions. The completed map includes measured-versus-predicted labels and uncertainty estimates, and turns a painstaking project that could consume weeks of human effort into one completed over a few days.

Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/the-missing-map-of-the-sky)

## Arduino’s edge-AI board passes 270,000 shipments

One year after Arduino joined Qualcomm, the organizations report more than 270,000 UNO Q boards shipped worldwide. The Linux-capable edge-AI board helped support more than 400 ecosystem events and 4,000 contest participants, while Arduino says Project Hub submissions nearly doubled and library downloads grew about 50% year over year.

Source: [Arduino](https://blog.arduino.cc/2026/10/08/one-year-of-uno-q-built-together-open-by-design/)

## TSMC’s September revenue jumps 54.6% year over year

TSMC reported September revenue of approximately NT$511.86 billion, down only 0.6% from August but up 54.6% from a year earlier. Revenue for January through September rose 44.3% year over year, another signal that demand for advanced manufacturing capacity remains intense amid the AI infrastructure buildout.

Source: [TSMC](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3343)

At the same time, it reiterated a ten-year, £11 billion program to build a nationwide 5G standalone, AI-ready network—an ambitious combination of post-merger consolidation and infrastructure investment.

## Research Radar

### OctoSense: Building a Unified Ecosystem for Open-Source Wireless Sensing

Weiying Hou, Xie Zhang, and Chenshu Wu propose a common data abstraction, standardized signal operators, and a benchmark engine for wireless sensing. The goal is an “ImageNet-style” ecosystem where datasets and models can be adapted with a few lines of declarative configuration instead of one-off pipelines.

Source: [arXiv:2610.12405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12405)

### Interference-Aware Downlink Power Control for High-Altitude Platform Stations with QoS and Max-Min Fairness

Rajan Shrestha and Hayder Al-Hraishawi derive tractable power-control schemes for HAPS links under 3GPP NTN path loss and Rician fading. Their QoS-oriented method reduces required transmit power by up to 6 dB in the tested regimes and approaches linear-programming performance while cutting average computation time by about 99.97%. The work is accepted for IEEE CCNC 2027.

Source: [arXiv:2610.12380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12380)

### An Experimental Evaluation of Scheduled Transmission and Hardware Timestamping on the Intel E830 NIC

Takahiro Hirofuchi experimentally evaluates the Intel E830 NIC with FPGA-based, nanosecond-resolution measurements. About 99% of observed frame intervals land within ±300 ns of target, suggesting utility for 5G fronthaul, though occasional outliers approaching 2 microseconds may be too loose for the strictest TSN use cases.

Source: [arXiv:2610.11589](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11589)
