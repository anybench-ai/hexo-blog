## Morning Digest — Saturday, August 8

### OpenAI flags Astra as its first “critical” cyber model
OpenAI says one of its upcoming models, Astra, is now being treated as the company’s first **critical** cybersecurity model under its Preparedness Framework. The significance is not just the model itself, but the admission that some systems now cross an internal threshold where ordinary launch discipline is no longer enough.

Source: [https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2085801349866729975](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2085801349866729975)

### Google DeepMind spotlights Apollo 2 running on Gemini Robotics 2
Google DeepMind shared a fresh look at Apollo 2 operating on Gemini Robotics 2. That makes the story more than a robotics demo: it shows a major model lab treating embodied agents as a first-class extension of its model stack.

Source: [https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2085737848318124083](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2085737848318124083)

### NVIDIA expands sovereign AI infrastructure push in Armenia and Kazakhstan
NVIDIA says Firebird Cloud AI is helping Armenia and Kazakhstan build local AI infrastructure powered by the NVIDIA DSX platform. The broader pattern is that compute clusters are increasingly being framed as national capability and industrial policy, not merely datacenter inventory.

Source: [https://x.com/nvidia/status/2086040693596659932](https://x.com/nvidia/status/2086040693596659932)

### Qualcomm says 3GPP has now made the 6G timeline official
Qualcomm highlighted that 3GPP Release 21 is now locked for **March 2029**, giving the 6G roadmap a firmer standards anchor. For anyone in wireless research, this matters because it sharpens the clock for pre-standard experiments, architecture bets, and commercialization planning.

Source: [https://x.com/Qualcomm/status/2085837175204036973](https://x.com/Qualcomm/status/2085837175204036973)

### Ericsson demos GNN-driven closed-loop healing for autonomous networks
Ericsson says a TM Forum Catalyst project used graph neural networks to identify faults early and trigger closed-loop healing. This is one of the more concrete AI-native networking stories of the day because it targets operator workflows rather than broad future-of-6G rhetoric.

Source: [https://x.com/ericsson/status/2085667283486007628](https://x.com/ericsson/status/2085667283486007628)

### SpaceX is still trying to recover Starship Flight 13 from the Indian Ocean
SpaceX says its recovery team is still working to bring back Flight 13’s Starship despite rough seas and difficult operating conditions. It is a useful reminder that reusable-space economics depend on recovery operations and logistics just as much as launch spectacle.

Source: [https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2085786988225925349](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2085786988225925349)

## Research Radar

### FedRings: A Scalable and Topology-Aware Federated Learning Framework for LEO Satellite Constellations
**Authors:** Ziwu Liu, Inês Pinto Gouveia, Rehana Yasmin, Paulo Esteves-Verissimo  
**Venue:** arXiv  
FedRings proposes a federated-learning framework that explicitly adapts to constellation topology, which makes it especially relevant for learning tasks spread across dynamic LEO graphs.

🔗 [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03436v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03436v1)

### LEO-Aware DRL Meta-Scheduler for 5G Non-Terrestrial Network Slicing
**Authors:** Víctor Vilchez, Tiago P. C. de Andrade, Edward Hinojosa, Edmundo Madeira  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper studies NTN slicing with a LEO-aware deep RL scheduler, making it directly relevant to hybrid terrestrial-satellite resource orchestration.

🔗 [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01668v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01668v1)

### When Agentic AI Meets Integrated Sensing and Communication
**Authors:** Kai Li, Conggai Li, Sarah Ali Siddiqui, Syed Sohail Ahmed  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper is interesting because it reframes ISAC as part of a larger agentic decision loop, not just a signal-processing or protocol problem.

🔗 [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05792v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05792v1)
