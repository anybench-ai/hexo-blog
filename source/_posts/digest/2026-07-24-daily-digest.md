## Morning Digest for Friday, July 24, 2026

### 1) OpenAI launches Health in ChatGPT for U.S. users
OpenAI is rolling out **Health in ChatGPT** to logged-in U.S. users 18 and older on web and iOS. Users can connect Apple Health and supported medical records so ChatGPT can answer questions with personal context, help compare new and prior results, and summarize changes over time. The privacy angle matters: OpenAI says connected medical records, Apple Health data, and conversations that use them are **not used to train foundation models or target ads**.

Source: https://openai.com/index/health-in-chatgpt/

### 2) ChatGPT Voice arrives in Work and Codex on desktop
According to OpenAI’s July 23 release notes, **ChatGPT Voice** is now available in **Work** and **Codex** inside the ChatGPT desktop app. That means users can speak naturally while the app coordinates tool use and agent workflows. The bigger signal is interface evolution: voice is becoming a control surface for multi-step agentic work rather than a simple conversational add-on.

Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes

### 3) Google makes the Gemini Interactions API generally available
Google announced that the **Interactions API** is now the primary API for working with Gemini models and agents. The generally available release adds a stable schema and formalizes capabilities like **server-side state**, **background execution**, and **managed-agent support**. For developers building long-running agents, this is a meaningful move from “preview-era experimentation” to a supported production interface.

Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/interactions-api-general-availability/

### 4) NVIDIA commissions a DGX GB300 at the Naval Postgraduate School
NVIDIA says Jensen Huang visited the **Naval Postgraduate School** in Monterey on July 22 to bring a **DGX GB300** system fully online for students and researchers. The story matters beyond one install: frontier AI infrastructure is increasingly being embedded into mission-driven universities and public-sector research environments, not just hyperscaler data centers.

Source: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/naval-postgraduate-school-dgx-ai-supercomputer/

### 5) SpaceX retargets Starship Flight 13 to Friday for better heatshield imagery
SpaceX said on July 23 that weather forced it to retarget **Starship’s thirteenth flight test** to **Friday, July 24, 2026**. The company highlighted a specific engineering goal: obtaining clearer ground imagery of Starship’s heatshield while the vehicle experiences higher dynamic pressure during ascent. That’s a useful reminder that the current Starship campaign is as much about instrumentation and learning as it is about simply getting off the pad.

Source: https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2080384481043656948

### 6) Amazon Leo says it now has 375+ satellites in orbit
Amazon’s **mission updates** page says the latest Atlas V deployment pushed **Amazon Leo** beyond **375 satellites in orbit**. That does not make it a peer to Starlink yet, but it does mark visible execution progress toward a real operational broadband constellation. For the LEO sector, the important signal is that Amazon is now accumulating orbital assets at a meaningful pace.

Source: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/innovation-at-amazon/project-kuiper-satellite-rocket-launch-progress-updates

### 7) Anthropic lets Claude answer questions about the Economic Index
Anthropic said Claude can now answer questions about the **Anthropic Economic Index**, its public dataset on how AI is used across the economy. This is a small but important productization step: a research dataset becomes more useful when users can interrogate it conversationally instead of reading charts and reports manually.

Source: https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2079980981264544017

## Research Radar

### Out-of-Distribution Detection in Wireless Multimodal Foundation Models for 6G ISAC
**Authors:** Mohammad Farzanullah, Akram Bin Sediq, Ali Afana, Melike Erol-Kantarci  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper tackles OOD detection for wireless multimodal foundation models in 6G ISAC settings, which is directly relevant if these systems are going to behave safely under real deployment shifts.

🔗 http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21455v1

### Large Language Model Assisted Intent-Based Satellite-Integrated Access and Backhaul FWA for Rural Areas
**Authors:** Anselme Ndikumana, Kim Khoa Nguyen, Adel Larabi, Mohamed Cheriet  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper explores using LLMs to orchestrate satellite-integrated access and backhaul for rural fixed wireless access, making it especially relevant to NTN service design and operational automation.

🔗 http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21272v1

### RadioTrace: Transmitter-Aware Diffusion for Radio Map Estimation without Deployment-Time Fine-Tuning
**Authors:** Liu Yang, Qiang Li, Zhuo Cao, Weijie Xiong, Guomin Sun, Jingran Lin  
**Venue:** arXiv  
The authors use diffusion methods for radio-map estimation without deployment-time fine-tuning, which could matter for scalable propagation modeling, planning, and measurement workflows.

🔗 http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20909v1
