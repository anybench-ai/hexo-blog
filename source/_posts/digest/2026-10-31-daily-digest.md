Today’s developments show AI competition expanding beyond model benchmarks into sovereign financing, enterprise privacy, radio infrastructure, orbital capacity, and control over how information moves across the web.

## Amazon Leo signs AT&T as its first major U.S. carrier partner

Amazon says its low-Earth-orbit broadband service remains on track to begin initial operations later this year. A strategic agreement makes AT&T the first major U.S. carrier set to offer Amazon Leo connectivity to enterprise and public-sector customers, combining satellite coverage with AT&T’s fiber and 5G services while the carrier handles equipment, installation, billing, and support. The arrangement gives Amazon an established distribution channel as it begins competing more directly with Starlink in managed connectivity.

Source: [Amazon Q3 2026 results](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)

## Nokia and NVIDIA push AI-RAN from platform launch toward deployment

Nokia and NVIDIA are moving their commercial AI-RAN platform into operator integration. BT, Deutsche Telekom, Elisa, and Orange are among the carriers progressing work that combines radio-access workloads, distributed accelerated computing, and agentic software on shared infrastructure. The practical near-term target is better 5G and 5G-Advanced performance; strategically, the same architecture is meant to become a bridge toward AI-native 6G and physical-AI services.

Source: [Telecom Review Asia](https://www.telecomreviewasia.com/news/network-news/30650-nokia-and-nvidia-advance-ai-native-networks-toward-deployment/)

## Mistral releases the weights for its trillion-parameter flagship

Mistral has released the weights for Mistral Large 4, code-named “Le Chonk.” The natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model contains one trillion total parameters with 52 billion active per token and is positioned for agentic coding and cybersecurity work. Releasing the weights turns a preview API model into infrastructure that researchers and companies can inspect, adapt, and deploy under their own controls.

Source: [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)

## OpenAI introduces stronger controls for sensitive enterprise data

OpenAI Private Intelligence is a new enterprise push centered on tighter controls over business data while customers use the company’s most capable models. OpenAI says it is developing the protections with large enterprise customers, reflecting a growing constraint on frontier-AI adoption: model capability alone is insufficient when workflows contain regulated, proprietary, or otherwise sensitive information.

Source: [OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2116186509384815084)

## Canada commits up to C$1.6 billion to Cohere

Canada plans to make a commercial equity investment of up to C$1.6 billion in Cohere alongside private investors including NVIDIA, Bell, and Schwarz Group. The government frames the deal as a sovereign-AI investment intended to expand secure domestic infrastructure and retain Canadian talent and economic value. It is also a striking example of states acting not only as regulators and buyers of AI, but as direct shareholders in strategically important model companies.

Source: [Prime Minister of Canada](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/10/29/canada-announces-landmark-investment-cohere-scale-secure-sovereign-ai)

## Reddit says Google’s AI search is cutting referral traffic

Reddit told shareholders that search referrals fell by more than one million per week on average during October after a meaningful decline in the third quarter. CEO Steve Huffman said Google’s AI Mode does not drive traffic, and Reddit is preparing to test a more login-gated experience. The episode makes the open-web tradeoff concrete: AI-generated answers can satisfy users without sending them to the communities and publishers that supplied the underlying material.

Source: [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/reddit-to-test-login-only-mode-as-google-search-traffic-drops/595270/)

## SpaceX launches 27 more Starlink satellites

SpaceX launched and deployed 27 additional Starlink satellites from California early Saturday. Although individual Starlink launches are now routine, the continuing cadence matters: constellation scale is a central competitive advantage as satellite broadband and direct-to-device services move toward broader commercial availability.

Source: [SpaceX](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2116401549828391177)

## Research Radar

### Federated Agentic AI for Interference-Aware Adaptive Spectrum Coordination

Tomer Baziza Paz, Yaniv Cohen, and Kobi Cohen propose FASCIA, a federated multi-agent reinforcement-learning framework in which local network managers learn spectrum policies from their own interference conditions and periodically aggregate models without exchanging raw sensing data. Simulations place its performance close to a centralized benchmark while preserving local data and handling partially overlapping channels.

Source: [arXiv:2610.39680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39680)

### From Human Intent to In-Orbit Action: A Self-Learning Large Language Model Agent for 6G Satellite Networks

Zhengqian Xu, Yasheng Zhang, Chenhua Sun, Shan Lu, and Chengcheng Li present ALMOND, which translates natural-language service intent into reinforcement-learning objectives and learns constraints from failed trajectories. In a 350-satellite digital twin, it raised polar handover success from 55% to over 95%, converged roughly three times faster than baseline reinforcement learning, and reduced reward-engineering work from 48 hours to 4.5 hours.

Source: [arXiv:2610.34387](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34387)

### Denial of Service Attacks on Beam Allocation in Large LEO Constellations

Ali Abbasi, Pasha Barahimi, Marco Paolieri, and Leana Golubchik analyze adversarial demand inflation against greedy and max-min-fair LEO beam allocation. Demand-proportional capacity sharing increased the fraction of underserved legitimate users by as much as 34% under attack, while equal sharing limited the increase to 13%, exposing a concrete robustness-versus-efficiency tradeoff for constellation schedulers.

Source: [arXiv:2610.34631](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34631)
