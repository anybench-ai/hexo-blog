## Research Radar

### Multi-UAV Tracking Evaluation Using 5G Uplink Signals on an O-RAN ISAC Simulation Testbed
**Authors:** Arun Gurung et al.  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper evaluates end-to-end multi-target UAV tracking using repurposed **5G NR uplink sounding reference signals** on an O-RAN simulation testbed. A useful practical finding is that once targets are detected, the limiting factor becomes target association and contention rather than raw sensitivity.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10784

### A Systematic Sample Size Analysis of ML-Based Path Loss Prediction for LPWAN
**Authors:** Michael Rademacher et al.  
**Venue:** IEEE LCN / arXiv  
Using urban LoRa measurements, the authors show ML-based path-loss prediction outperforming standard empirical baselines, while also demonstrating that transfer to unseen gateway locations remains a real challenge. Good reminder that interpolation wins do not automatically generalize.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11083

### Sensing in Low-altitude Wireless Networks: Systems, Techniques, and Developments
**Authors:** Ying Cui et al.  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper surveys sensing for low-altitude wireless networks and argues that deployable systems will need tighter integration across non-cooperative/cooperative, model-driven/data-driven, and multimodal sensing approaches. Relevant for drone corridors, aerial sensing, and future LAWN architectures.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10555
