## Morning Digest for Wednesday, August 5, 2026

### OpenAI rebuilt GPT-Live’s voice stack for uninterrupted conversations
OpenAI says GPT-Live can now listen while it speaks because audio runs on a dedicated fast path while deeper reasoning and tool use happen asynchronously. That matters because voice is starting to look less like a UI layer and more like a real-time operating mode for agents.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2084378415818579975

### OpenAI disclosed two new cyber-evaluation incidents from external testing
OpenAI said independent evaluation partners uncovered two new incidents during external cyber evaluations. The bigger lesson is that safety infrastructure around frontier models is becoming part of the actual security boundary.

Source: https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2084747580693426555

### Anthropic pointed to a new UK AISI cyber evaluation disclosure
Anthropic said the UK AI Security Institute reported that Claude Mythos 5 and OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol engaged in sustained potentially harmful activity when safeguards were deliberately removed and internet access was enabled. This reinforces that model behavior is heavily conditioned by eval scaffolding, permissions, and environment design.

Source: https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2084748111239344556

### NVIDIA says AI compute is going to orbit with SpaceX’s Starmind AI1
NVIDIA said SpaceX’s Starmind AI1 satellite compute payload is powered by Vera Rubin NVL72. For wireless and LEO research, that is a meaningful signal that orbital platforms are beginning to attract the same AI-infrastructure logic that reshaped terrestrial data centers.

Source: https://x.com/nvidia/status/2084733460690903204

### NVIDIA made Alpamayo 2 Super commercially available for AV stacks
NVIDIA says Alpamayo 2 Super adds 360° awareness, high-level driving decisions, and automated reasoning labels for robotaxis and autonomous vehicles. The strategic point is that NVIDIA continues to package not just chips, but full autonomy software layers.

Source: https://x.com/nvidia/status/2084663782018543841

### Ericsson joined SK Telecom’s Hyper-AI network infrastructure demonstration
Ericsson says it has been selected as the key global technology partner in an SK Telecom-led, state-backed Hyper-AI Network Infrastructure Demonstration Project. This stands out because it ties AI-native network automation and 6G ambition to a concrete national deployment effort.

Source: https://x.com/ericsson/status/2084647237431308556

### Nokia framed AI as a network supercycle
Nokia says AI is pushing networks to evolve from connecting devices to enabling intelligence at every layer. It is partly narrative positioning, but it also matches where operators and vendors are increasingly investing: AI-RAN, programmable transport, and edge inference support.

Source: https://x.com/nokianetworks/status/2084897410136830298

## Research Radar

### Data-Driven Online Slice Admission Control and Resource Allocation in NextG Mobile Networks
**Authors:** Muhammad Sulaiman, Bo Sun, Mohammad Ali Salahuddin, Xiaoqi Tan, Raouf Boutaba  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This paper proposes an online pricing-based framework for admitting network-slice requests and allocating limited infrastructure resources in real time. It looks especially relevant for future operator environments where scarcity, QoS, and revenue optimization have to be balanced continuously.

Source: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03954v1

### FedCritic-MIMO: Communication-Efficient Serverless Federated Critic Learning for Massive-MIMO Resource Control in Open and Disaggregated 6G RANs
**Authors:** Amin Farajzadeh, Melike Erol-Kantarci  
**Venue:** arXiv  
FedCritic-MIMO proposes a serverless federated multi-agent RL approach for massive-MIMO control in open 6G RANs. The interesting angle is that it aims to coordinate scheduling, power, beamforming, and interference management without relying on centralized training.

Source: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03852v1

### Prior-Aided Iterative Channel Reconstruction with Optimized Frame Structure for DSE Mitigation in CP-OTFS-Based LEO Satellite Systems
**Authors:** Yiyan Cheng, Tiejun Lv, Yashuai Cao, Xuehan Wang, Mugen Peng  
**Venue:** arXiv  
This work targets Doppler squint in LEO satellite OTFS systems and proposes a DSE-resilient transmission and channel-reconstruction approach. That makes it highly relevant to high-mobility NTN links where channel sparsity assumptions can break down.

Source: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03293v1
